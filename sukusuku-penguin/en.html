How to play

Tap FEED to help your penguin grow and change its appearance. End feeding to keep your record, or try another bite. See “How to play” in the app for details.

Frequently asked questions

When can I use AUTO?

AUTO unlocks after 10,000 total spins. Premium also unlocks AUTO, and double-speed AUTO is a Premium feature.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then choose “Restore purchases” under “How to play.” Restoring purchases does not restore your game records.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is a one-time purchase. Check the app and Apple’s purchase confirmation screen for the current price and features.

Where are my records saved?

Progress, personal bests, secrets, titles and growth images are stored on your device. Deleting the app may erase these records. The app does not provide its own cloud sync.

Why can’t I hear any sound?

Check SOUND in the app, your device volume, and connected headphones or Bluetooth devices.
