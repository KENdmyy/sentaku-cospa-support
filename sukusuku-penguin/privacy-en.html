Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

KENdmyy Apps (the developer) handles user information in the iOS app Sukusuku Penguin as described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, personal bests and history, penguin secrets, titles, growth images, total spins, settings and advertising frequency information are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a dedicated server or account registration system to view these records. Deleting the app may erase local records. Backups managed by the operating system or the user may be handled separately.

2. Game Center

The leaderboard feature sends scores to Apple Game Center. Your Game Center display name and scores may be visible to other players through Apple’s services. Apple manages Game Center accounts and data. See Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Google and advertising partners may process approximate location inferred from your IP address, device and app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app activity, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and diagnostics.

The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean that no information is processed for advertising. Consent and advertising settings are presented where applicable. When available, use “Ad privacy settings” under “How to play” to change your choices. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases, restoration and purchase verification use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Verified purchases enable Premium features including ad removal, AUTO and double speed. The developer does not receive credit card numbers or other payment details.

5. Contact inquiries

When you email us, we receive your reply address, message and optional attachments. We use them as needed to respond and investigate issues. Please do not send personal information that is unnecessary for support. You may also contact us to request deletion of inquiry information.

6. Support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add our own analytics or advertising scripts. Information handled by the hosting platform is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. Changes and contact

We update this policy and its date when features or information handling change.
